02 — ABOUT NVIDIA

Understanding NVIDIA Trading Data

NVIDIA is a major semiconductor and technology company known for its graphics processing units and artificial intelligence hardware.

Historical NVIDIA stock data is used in this project to demonstrate how regression algorithms can learn relationships between trading features and the closing price.
