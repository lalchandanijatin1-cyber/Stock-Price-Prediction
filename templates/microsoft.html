02 — ABOUT MICROSOFT

Understanding Microsoft Trading Data

Microsoft is a major technology company whose shares are actively traded in financial markets.

This project uses historical Microsoft market data to demonstrate how regression algorithms can learn relationships between trading features and the closing price.

Open

Microsoft stock price at the beginning of the selected trading period.

High

Highest Microsoft stock price during the selected period.

Low

Lowest Microsoft stock price during the selected period.

Volume

Amount of Microsoft stock traded during the selected period.

Close

The closing price that the model attempts to estimate.
