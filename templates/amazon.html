02 — ABOUT AMAZON

Understanding Amazon Trading Data

Amazon is a major global technology and e-commerce company whose stock is actively traded in financial markets.

Historical Amazon market data is used in this project to demonstrate how machine learning regression algorithms can learn relationships between trading features and closing prices.

Open

Amazon stock price at the beginning of the selected trading period.

High

Highest Amazon stock price recorded during the selected period.

Low

Lowest Amazon stock price recorded during the selected period.

Volume

Amount of Amazon stock traded during the selected period.

Close

The closing price that the machine learning model attempts to estimate.
