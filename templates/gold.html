02 — ABOUT GOLD

Understanding Gold Trading Data

Gold is a globally traded commodity and is often considered an important financial asset. Its market price changes continuously according to supply, demand, economic conditions, market sentiment and other external factors.

In this project, historical gold trading data is used as the foundation for machine learning. The model learns relationships between the input trading features and the closing price contained in the dataset.

Open

The price at which trading began for the selected trading period.

High

The highest recorded trading price during the selected period.

Low

The lowest recorded trading price during the selected period.

Volume

The amount of trading activity recorded during the selected period.

Close

The closing price is the target that the machine learning model attempts to predict.
