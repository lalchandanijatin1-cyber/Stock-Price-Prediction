02 — ABOUT APPLE

Understanding Apple Trading Data

Apple is one of the world's largest technology companies and its stock is actively traded in financial markets.

In this project, historical Apple stock data is used to demonstrate how machine learning regression algorithms can learn relationships between historical trading features and the closing price.

Open

The price at which Apple stock began trading during the selected period.

High

The highest Apple stock price recorded during the selected period.

Low

The lowest Apple stock price recorded during the selected period.

Volume

The amount of Apple stock traded during the selected period.

Close

The closing price that the machine learning model attempts to estimate.
